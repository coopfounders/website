Coop

The autonomous operations platform for hospitals.

Hospitals today run pharmacy robots, specimen couriers, inventory bots, and cleaning machines alongside human transport teams — each operating in isolation, with nothing above them to coordinate the whole. Coop is that layer. It orchestrates robots, staff, equipment, and inventory in real time, eliminating the fragmented handoffs and idle assets that cost hospitals thousands of hours each year.
