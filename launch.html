Hospitals have the technology.
Now it is time to unlock its potential.

Hospitals have invested in some of the most advanced technology in the world. Yet much of its potential remains untapped.

The problem is not the ambition of hospital teams or the capability of the technology. It is the fragmented coordination surrounding how these systems fit into daily operations. Too often, clinicians and operators are left bridging those gaps themselves.

At Coop, we are working toward a future where hospital teams can get far more from the technology around them. Advanced systems should meaningfully expand what people can accomplish, help hospitals automate manual workflows, and enable them to operate better for everyone who depends on healthcare.
