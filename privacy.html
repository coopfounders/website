Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

01 Overview

Coop ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

02 Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact information you provide through forms (name, email, phone, company)
  • Information about your operation submitted through our pilot interest form
  • Usage data from our website (pages visited, time on site)
  • Sensor data collected from barns during pilot deployments (with your permission)

03 How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and process pilot applications
  • To improve our products and services
  • To send you updates you have opted into
  • To improve our monitoring models (only with your permission, using aggregated and anonymized data)

04 Data ownership

You own your data. Sensor data collected from your barns belongs to you. We ask permission to use aggregated and anonymized data to improve our models. You can revoke this permission at any time.

05 Data security

We use encryption in transit and at rest. Access to data is role-based and limited to authorized personnel. We follow industry practices for data protection.

06 Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at founders@cooplabs.com.
